Welcome

CallPilot helps you plan important calls, organize contacts into groups, set reminders, and prepare short voice messages. Everything stays on your device — no account required.

Use the main tabs for Plans, Contacts, Groups, and Voice Messages. Allow notifications so reminders can reach you on time.

Getting started

  • Complete onboarding and grant the permissions you need (contacts, notifications, microphone).
  • Import or add contacts you want to call regularly.
  • Create your first call plan with a date and time.

You can deny a permission and still use other features. CallPilot will show how to enable it later in Settings.

Call plans & reminders

Create a plan for a contact or group, set when you want to be reminded, and track status as you go.

  • When a reminder fires, use Call, Snooze, or Dismiss.
  • Snooze options typically include 5, 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
  • Mark plans as called, completed, missed, or cancelled as needed.

Contacts

Browse and manage the people you call. With contacts permission, you can import from your device address book.

  • Open a contact to start a plan or place a call via the system dialer.
  • Keep notes and context that help you remember why the call matters.

Groups

Organize contacts into groups — for example family, clients, or a project team.

  • Create a group and add members.
  • Plan calls against a whole group when you need a sequence or shared follow-up.

Voice messages

Record a short message (up to 25 seconds) and schedule a guided workflow for when to play it.

  • Grant microphone permission when recording.
  • At reminder time, CallPilot guides you: call → play on speaker → continue to the next recipient.

Mobile platforms do not allow third-party apps to inject audio into a live cellular call. CallPilot uses an honest guided flow instead of pretending unsupported automation succeeded.

Calling & conferences

  • CallPilot opens your phone’s dialer (or places a call when permission and platform allow).
  • For conferences, follow the in-app guidance and use your Phone app’s Merge Calls control.

Settings & permissions

Theme

  • Choose light, dark, or system theme.

Reminders

  • Keep notification and exact-alarm permissions enabled for reliable reminders on Android.
  • Set a default snooze duration.

Permissions

  • Contacts — import people.
  • Notifications — call and voice reminders.
  • Microphone — record voice messages.
  • Phone — optional direct calling where supported.

Privacy

CallPilot is local-first: no login and no CallPilot cloud sync. Plans, contacts you manage in-app, and recordings stay on your device. See the full Privacy Policy.

Tips & contact

  • Allow notifications so reminders are not delayed by battery savers.
  • Keep the app updated from the store for fixes and improvements.
  • Share the install page: Get CallPilot.
